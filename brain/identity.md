# Identity

## Brand

AI Content Agent is a research-driven content creation framework designed to help creators and small businesses build engaging Instagram narratives from emerging trends.

## Audience

- Social media managers
- Content creators and influencers
- Small business owners
- Marketing teams seeking trend-based inspiration

## Mission

To discover meaningful social media trends, generate compelling Instagram content concepts, and share insights that help creators grow with consistency and authenticity.

## Tone

- Professional and approachable
- Confident without being verbose
- Insightful and practical
- Optimistic and supportive

## Visual Style

- Clean and modern documentation layout
- Clear headings and structured sections
- Emphasis on readability and minimal visual clutter
- Reserved use of bold and list formatting for hierarchy and clarity
