# Editorial strategy

## Positioning

Make Aimagix the account that turns familiar Indian products into short, original, cinematic ad concepts and shows how each concept was made. The creative should look like a studio portfolio, while the process should make it useful to founders and creators.

## Four weekly slots

1. **Spec ad / product transformation (2 posts):** A fictional product or a real product used with permission. Show the product clearly and make the idea understandable without sound.
2. **Concept breakdown (1 post):** Show brief → visual direction → generated assets → edit. Explain one specific decision or limitation.
3. **Original visual story (1 post):** An original character or world with a product/brand-design lesson. Anime influence is acceptable; named franchise characters and copied artist styles are not.

## Format and cadence

- Start with four feed posts per week for 30 days: three Reels and one carousel or still breakdown.
- Reels should generally be 8–18 seconds, with a clear visual change in the first second, a simple middle, and a payoff or loop.
- Add two or three lightweight Story frames on non-posting days: polls, prompt choices, work-in-progress, or a question about a real design decision.
- Schedule in IST, but treat the initial slot as a test. Compare performance by format and day after the sample has accumulated; do not assume a universal “best time.”

## Creative rules

- Every post needs one audience promise: a surprising product transformation, a useful creative lesson, or a memorable original story.
- Do not publish an image just because it is attractive. Show what the studio made, why the choice works, or how a viewer can use the idea.
- Use original fictional packaging or client-provided assets. Label spec ads as “concept / not affiliated” in the caption when a real brand is referenced.
- Avoid unsupported claims, generic motivational aphorisms, repetitive lone-hero scenes, fake testimonials, and invented campaign results.
- Use 3–5 specific hashtags at most; include searchable product/category terms in the spoken or on-screen wording and caption.
- Never ask a model to draw a logo or exact text. Render typography and logos as editable overlays after image generation.

## Learning rules

- Run automated pre-publish checks. Repair a recoverable defect; if the asset still fails, skip that post and continue the schedule without waiting for a person.
- Record the creative hypothesis, format, hook, duration, topic, and one variable changed per experiment.
- Compare medians across at least four posts per format before changing the strategy. Treat small samples as directional, not proof.
- Prioritize non-follower reach, average watch time/retention, shares per reach, saves per reach, profile visits per reach, and follows per profile visit.
- Do not hard-code “viral” thresholds. Use this account's own baseline and report missing metrics as missing.
