# Identity

## Brand

Anime AI India is a research-driven Instagram content brand that publishes about artificial intelligence and anime for the Indian audience. One day it covers AI — tools, news, career tips — and the next day anime — recommendations, seasonal releases, culture, and AI-generated anime art.

## Audience

- Indian Instagram users aged 16-34 across Tier 1, Tier 2, and Tier 3 cities
- Anime fans (huge and growing in India) and AI-curious students, developers, freelancers, and small creators
- Hinglish-first, mobile-first, value-driven scroll consumers
- Students prepping for exams/careers, young professionals, and anime community members

## Mission

To make AI feel approachable and anime feel shareable for Indian Instagram, one short, relatable post at a time — alternating daily between the two worlds.

## Tone

- Enthusiastic but practical ("kaam ki baat")
- Relatable, meme-adjacent where appropriate
- Simple over technical; explain jargon in one line
- Respectful of anime fandoms and honest about AI hype
- Optimistic and supportive

## Visual Style

- Bold, colorful, high-contrast carousel covers
- Clean typography, minimal clutter, one idea per slide
- Anime-inspired color palettes for anime days; futuristic/tech gradients for AI days
- Strong scroll-stopping first slide with a question or bold claim
